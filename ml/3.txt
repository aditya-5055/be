3. StandardScaler
Q15. What is StandardScaler?

It is used to bring numerical features to a similar scale.

Q16. Why do we use StandardScaler?

Because features have different ranges. Scaling helps the neural network train more effectively.

Q17. What is the formula of StandardScaler?
\[
Z=\frac{X-\text{Mean}}{\text{Standard Deviation}}
\]

Q18. Why do we use fit_transform() on training data?

Q28. What is Sigmoid?
